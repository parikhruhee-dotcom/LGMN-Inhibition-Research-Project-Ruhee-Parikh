# Selectivity against related cysteine proteases

Generated by `src/data/selectivity.py`.

Legumain is a clan CD cysteine protease, the same clan as the caspases, and 85%
of the compounds here carry a warhead aimed at a catalytic cysteine. A legumain
inhibitor that also hits caspase-3 -- the executioner protease of apoptosis --
is not a drug candidate. This was listed as an untested limitation of the
project; what follows is what the public data can and cannot say.

## Coverage

- Compounds in the modelling set: **180**
- With any published counter-screen among the targets tested here: **4**
- Never counter-screened: **176**

## Results

Selectivity is pIC50(legumain) minus pIC50(off-target): positive means the
compound prefers legumain. Below 1 log unit (10-fold) there is no
therapeutic window to optimise from.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Caspase-3 | CD | 4 | +3.23 | 3/4 | 0 |

### Measurements versus lower bounds

Of 14 compound-target pairs, **12 are lower bounds**, not
measurements: the record says the off-target was not inhibited up to the highest
concentration tested, which here is uniformly 50 uM. For those the true
selectivity could be anything from the stated figure upwards, and the number in
the table is the floor.

Only **2 pairs** carry an actual off-target IC50, from a single compound.

This distinction matters more than it looks. 'No inhibition at 50 uM' against a
panel is a weaker claim than a titrated counter-screen: it rules out potent
off-target activity and says nothing about moderate activity, which is what
usually causes trouble in vivo.


**0 compounds** show less than 10-fold separation from at
least one counter-target.

## Caveats

- Counter-screen data is published selectively: a compound tested and found
  unselective is less likely to be reported than one that looked clean, so these
  numbers are optimistic.
- A `>` record means no inhibition up to the highest concentration tested. Those
  are kept as evidence *for* selectivity but understate the true ratio.
- Absence of a counter-screen is not selectivity. 176 compounds have no
  data at all and should be treated as unknown, not as safe.
